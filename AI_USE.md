# Use of AI in Open Source SAR

*Maintained by Mind (Ravada Siddharth), team lead. NASA Space Apps asks teams to mark where AI ("Super Intelligence") tools were used. This file is that statement for the repository.*

**Tool:** Claude, by Anthropic (an AI assistant).

| Part of the project | How AI was used |
| --- | --- |
| Planning | Used in our first planning conversation to explore the challenge and brainstorm what to build. The team set the goals and rules: global scope, free and open source only, a ten-part build, honest labelling of demo data, and the name. |
| Code (`pipeline/`, `web/`, `server/`, `.github/`) | Most code, tests and workflows were written by the assistant under our direction. We ran, reviewed and debugged them on our own machines, including against real NISAR files. |
| Text (README, DEPLOY, ROADMAP, SUBMISSION, `data/explain.json`, `data/stories.json`) | Drafted by the assistant, including the Hindi and Spanish explanations, and edited by the team. |
| Synthetic demo data | Generated by our own code (the demo generators in `pipeline/`), not by an AI model. Every synthetic layer is labelled "Synthetic demo" in the app and has `"synthetic": true` in its manifest. |
| Images, audio, video | None are AI-generated. Screenshots are of the real app. |
| The running app | Uses no AI. Explanations are filled in from templates that only restate numbers in the data. |

No NASA branding or design elements were used or modified.
